The Protocol

$FAMR is the first Incubator Project launching on the LiquidityBooster dApp for full-scale integration.

Mint

Acquire tiered NFTs through the decentralized minting interface. Each NFT represents a unique yield-generating position within the ecosystem.

Stake

Lock NFTs to activate miting authority and generate hourly $FAMR rewards based on it's rarity.

Burn

Permanently destroy NFTs to receive instant burn rewards from the 2.43 Trillion $FAMR allocation pool.

Trade

Exchange NFTs on secondary markets with embedded rarity value and attributed staking potentials.

Everything you need to know about FamrChain Asset and the ecosystem.

FamrChain tokens digitizes Farm Livestocks produce using surreal NFTs motion arts on Avax C-Chain, and will soon become it's own native EVM. An Onchain Fchain.NFT stake or burn attracts $FAMR rewards.

Here's how it works simply:

  • Investors mints a limited ERC1155 standard NFTs as low as 4.00 AVAX. These funds supports protocol liquidity as its abstracted through a community multisig, for liquidity boosting.
  • These NFTs can earn $FAMR into owners wallet when staked.
  • Fchain.NFTs can also be burnt to earn $FAMR.

In future development, protocol shall combine Real farming + Its Token utility EVM + Scarcity model.

Simple steps:

  • Connect your wallet (Rabby Wallet or TokenPocket Wallet / MetaMask on AVAX C-Chain)
  • Mint a Fchain.NFT (limited supply)
  • Stake your NFT on the LiquidityBooster dApp
  • Receive farming-based reward distributions
  • Optionally, burn Fchain.NFT for burn rewards

NFT holders are positioned for: Farming profit rewards, Ecosystem incentives, Long-term value growth.

The burn mechanism permanently removes Fchain.NFTs from circulation.

When NFTs are burned, Staking frequency is reduced and scarcity increases.

Out of the 1 Quadrillion max supply, 80% - 800 Trillion $FAMR is lock and/or can be burnt parmanently or used for future development. This creates strong scarcity from early stage and prevents future market dilution.
